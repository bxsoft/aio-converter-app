Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AIO Converter ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. In short: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

1. Information we do not collect

AIO Converter does not:

  • Require an account, sign-in, or registration
  • Collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
  • Access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files
  • Use analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking tools
  • Show advertisements or use advertising identifiers
  • Sell, rent, or share any data with third parties

2. Data stored on your device

To make the app work, some data is saved locally on your device only. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

Data Purpose
Preferences (language, theme, decimal precision, digit grouping) Remember your settings between sessions
Favorite categories and last-used units Speed up your conversions
Conversion history (up to 100 recent entries) Let you review past conversions
Latest currency exchange rates Allow currency conversion while offline

You can clear your conversion history at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data from your device settings, permanently deletes all of the data above.

3. Network requests

The app connects to the internet only for the following purposes:

  • Currency exchange rates. When you use the currency converter, the app downloads current exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request contains no personal information. As with any internet request, the provider can see your IP address. See their privacy policy for details.
  • App updates. The app may check for and download updates through Expo's update service. These requests include technical information such as the platform and app version, but no personal information. See Expo's privacy policy.

All other features, including unit conversions, work fully offline.

4. Children's privacy

AIO Converter does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

5. Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data on our side to be exposed. Data stored locally is protected by your device's own security features.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the revised policy.
